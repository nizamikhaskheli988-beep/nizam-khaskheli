About

A journalist-turned-strategic-communications professional with 9+ years across broadcast news, digital media, and nonprofit donor communications.

I built a five-year national newsroom career across six broadcast and digital outlets — GTV Network, Pakistan Press Foundation, Asia Net, KTN News, Awaz TV, and Royal Karachi News — including a production and research role on the Charna Island Marine Life Documentary, a nominee among 32 international entries.
